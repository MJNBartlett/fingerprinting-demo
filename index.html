Understanding Fingerprinting

Discover how websites identify you without cookies and what data your browser reveals.

See Your Fingerprint Learn How It Works

What is it?

Browser fingerprinting is a technique used to identify and track users based on unique configurations of their browser and device, rather than cookies. It creates a digital signature that is surprisingly unique to you.

How does it work?

Websites collect data like your screen resolution, installed fonts, browser version, and even how your computer renders graphics. When combined, these data points create a unique ID that persists across sessions.

Why care?

Unlike cookies, fingerprints are hard to delete. They allow persistent tracking across sessions and even incognito modes, impacting your privacy. Awareness is the first step to protection.
